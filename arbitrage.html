Arbitrage windows close fast, often in minutes. The saved snapshot is usually hours old, so fetch live odds before placing anything. Live fetches cost about 4 API credits per game. Odds loaded on either page carry over to the other in this tab.

How it works: for each player, market and line, the best Over and best Under across your selected books are paired. When the two implied probabilities add up to less than 100%, splitting the stake as shown pays the same whichever side wins. Set the total to split, or set the Over or Under bet and the other side is sized to match. Return is profit as a share of the total stake. Novig prices also show Novig's win-probability % in parentheses.

Before you bet: confirm both prices are still live and the line matches exactly. Check each book's limits and its rules for players who don't play. Odds marked in orange haven't updated in over 15 minutes.

Placing the bets: Add to bet slip opens the book with that bet already selected, where the book supports it. Otherwise the button opens the market, the game or the book's homepage. Books don't let links fill in the amount, so tap Copy and paste the stake into the slip. Links appear once odds are fetched after this update.
