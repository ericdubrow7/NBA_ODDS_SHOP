Best prices come only from these. The fair price always uses every book.

The backtest used prices about 30 minutes before tip-off, so fetch live odds close to game time. Live fetches cost about 4 API credits per game. Odds loaded on any page carry over to the others in this tab.

How it works: each book's Over and Under prices are turned into a chance of winning with its margin (vig) removed, and those chances are averaged across every book quoting the line. That average is the fair %, and fair odds are the odds that break even at it. Edge is the expected profit per $1 at the best price if the fair % is right: fair % × best decimal odds − 1.

The rules: Edge > 2%; 4+ books quoting that exact line; Main line, the line most books quote for that player and stat; and Novig not best, meaning the best price is at a regular sportsbook. A Recommended pick meets the first three; rows shaded green also meet the fourth. Prices outside 1.02–50 decimal and two-sided quotes with over 15% margin are ignored as stale.

Placing a bet: the button next to the best odds opens that bet at the book, on your bet slip where the book supports it. Confirm the price and line are still there, and log every pick so this season's test keeps growing.
